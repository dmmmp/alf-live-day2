I’m Chandru — a weight-loss coach who believes lasting change should give you more life, not take it away.

My approach

Health is a practice,
not a punishment.

There’s no perfect meal plan or magic 30-day reset. Real progress comes from small choices repeated with patience — and a plan that works on busy Mondays just as well as it does on motivated ones.

My job is to make that plan clear, flexible, and yours. We’ll look at the whole picture: food, movement, sleep, stress, and the mindset that ties them together.
